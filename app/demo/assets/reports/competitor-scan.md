# Competitor scan: task boards for small teams

Five products looked at over two days. Prices are per seat per month.

## At a glance

| Product | Price | Free tier | Standout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board One | $8 | 3 users | fast keyboard UI |
| Kanbanly | $6 | unlimited users, 1 board | templates |
| Shiplog | $12 | none | built-in changelog |
| TaskTide | $5 | 5 users | calendar view |
| Plain Lists | $0 | everything | open source |

## Gaps we could own

1. Nobody does a good "what changed since I last looked" view.
2. Mobile apps are an afterthought across the board.

## Risks

- Plain Lists is free and decent; we need a clear reason to pay.
