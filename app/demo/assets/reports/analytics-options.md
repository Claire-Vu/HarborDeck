# Privacy-friendly analytics

Goal: page views, sign-up funnel and referrers without cookies or a consent banner.

## Short list

- **Self-hosted counter**: free, a tiny container, no funnels.
- **Hosted service A**: $9/mo up to 10k views, funnels included, EU hosting.
- **Hosted service B**: $14/mo, funnels plus goals, public dashboard option.

## Recommendation

Hosted service A. It covers the funnel we care about (landing → sign-up → first project) and fits the budget.

## Not chosen

Full product analytics suites: they need a consent banner and cost more than the whole budget.
