# Launch pricing: two shapes

## Option A: one plan

- $7 per seat per month, everything included.
- Simple to explain; no upgrade path to sell.

## Option B: free + pro

- Free up to 3 users and 2 boards.
- Pro $9 per seat per month with history, integrations and export.
- Expected conversion from the comparable products: 3 to 5 percent.

## Numbers

| Shape | Month-3 revenue (est.) | Support load |
|---|---|---|
| One plan | $1,100 | low |
| Free + pro | $1,600 | medium |

## Note

The free tier adds hosting cost of about $40/mo at the projected sign-ups.
